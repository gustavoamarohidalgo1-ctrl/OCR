ANDROID SYSTEM LIBRARIES, INCLUDING ZLIB
The release CMake links libpng and Leptonica to Android's system z library,
and uses platform libraries such as log, dl and jnigraphics. No separate zlib
binary is packaged in this Tesseract4Android AAR; its implementation/version
is provided by the Android operating system. System-library source and license
information is maintained by Android and the upstream zlib project:
https://android.googlesource.com/platform/external/zlib/
https://zlib.net/zlib_license.html
